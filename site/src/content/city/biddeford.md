## Why shared walls change the job

In a detached house, a bed bug or cockroach problem belongs to one household. In a three-decker it belongs to the building. Bed bugs travel along pipe chases, under doors and through wall voids, and they ride between apartments in laundry, bags and secondhand furniture. German cockroaches follow the plumbing runs that kitchens and bathrooms on different floors share.

That is why we inspect the units sharing a wall, a floor or a ceiling with the one that called, and treat every confirmed unit in the same window. The most comparable job on this site is a property-management call in Waterville: one unit reported bed bugs, the inspection found a large infestation with activity in connecting units, and the affected units were treated until it was gone. Doing it one unit at a time, as each tenant complains, costs a building more over a year and wears out everyone's patience.

## The law on bed bugs in a Maine rental

With most Biddeford households renting, the state statute matters here more than in most of the county. Section 6021-A of Title 14 of the Maine Revised Statutes sets out who does what:

- A landlord told about possible bed bugs, in writing or orally, has five days to inspect the unit.
- Once bed bugs are confirmed, the landlord has ten days to contact a pest control agent — defined as a certified commercial pesticide applicator — and must use one that carries current liability insurance.
- Before renting a unit, the landlord must tell a prospective tenant if an adjacent unit is infested or being treated.
- The tenant must report promptly, give access on reasonable notice, and follow reasonable control measures; a tenant who unreasonably fails to comply can be held financially responsible for the treatments that failure causes.

## Mice in the older stock

A building put up before 1940 has been re-plumbed, re-wired and re-heated several times, and every one of those jobs made a hole through the shell. In a multi-unit building there are more of them, and the basement is often shared, so mice that get in at the sill can reach every floor through the walls.

Rodent work in a Biddeford building therefore starts in the basement and at the foundation: sill gaps, bulkhead doors, the openings around water, gas and electrical entries, and the gap under any exterior door that has worn back. Each opening goes on a list with what should close it, and the sealing is quoted separately from the trapping. See [rodent exclusion](/services/rodent-exclusion/) for how that work is itemized and [rodent control](/services/rodent-control/) for the trapping side. ## Ticks near the beaches and the river

Tick work in Biddeford is mostly on the detached houses toward the coast and the city's edges, where a lawn meets brush. The UMaine Cooperative Extension Tick Lab tested 318 blacklegged ticks from York County in 2025 and found the Lyme bacterium in 133 of them, 42.0%, a hair above the 41.0% across every Maine tick it tested that year. Those are ticks people chose to send in rather than a random survey, so they show the bacterium is well established in the county rather than anyone's personal odds.
