Auburn sits across the Androscoggin from Lewiston and behaves differently. It is more owner-occupied, more suburban in layout, and heavier on single-family homes with attached garages and outbuildings — and the pest work reflects that.

## The garage is usually the way in

We did a whole-home inspection here where several entry points turned up, and the significant one was the garage door seals at the point where the garage joins the house. The seals were replaced, exterior bait stations went in where the travel routes were, and the problem stopped.

That is not an unusual finding in Auburn, it is the normal one. The bottom seal on an overhead door wears at the corners first, and a quarter-inch gap at the corner of a garage door is an open invitation in October. Once an animal is in the garage it is already inside the building envelope, and the interior door into the house is rarely sealed as well as the front door is.

## What a whole-home inspection covers here

- Garage door bottom and side seals, and the door from the garage into the house
- Sill line and foundation, including the bulkhead
- Utility penetrations: water service, electrical entry, dryer vent, hose bibs
- Roofline: soffit returns, drip edge, gable and ridge vent screening
- Outbuildings, and anything stored against an exterior wall
- Grounds: woodpiles, feeders, compost, and sheds sitting on soil

## Carpenter ants and the rest

Auburn's older neighbourhoods produce the same carpenter ant work as Lewiston — colonies in sills and window framing where water has been getting in. Tick pressure is higher here than in the denser parts of the corridor, because more properties back onto woods or field edges.

Tick and mosquito work runs monthly from April to September, which is the window when it does anything useful.
